How it works

Where each number comes from

Class combat runs on MythicSim's Forever engine (MIT licence), built from their repository at commit b3c5eb6 and running in your browser. On top of it, this page adds fixes for pets below level 60 and levels 1 to 9, the character sheet from the Forever client tables, talents from published leveling routes, typical gear for each level, the questing loop and the upgrade ranking.

Questing: the engine fights a normal mob of your level and gives damage per second and mana used. Kill time is the mob's health from the client tables divided by that. You take the mob's damage after your armor, dodge and parry while it is on you. Ranged classes get 4 seconds before it arrives, and a Hunter's or Warlock's pet takes the hits from level 10. After each kill you walk 10 seconds, then eat and drink together for what is still missing, with vendor food and water for your level. Healers quest with a damage rotation and their own talents.

Dungeon: damage specs fight a boss two levels above you for 2 minutes; healers run the engine's healer rotation. Tanks run as damage for now.

Not modelled: pulls with several mobs, kiting, loot and travel time, health regeneration outside eating, group buffs, enchants and consumables.
