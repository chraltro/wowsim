    Buy on the auction house, sell to a vendor

    Any listing under the vendor price is a sure profit. A scan gives only the cheapest and the median listing, so when the median is above the vendor price, fewer than half the listings qualify.

      Buy materials, craft, sell to a vendor

      A vendor pays more for these than their materials cost. Tap one for the bill and a shopping list.

        Buy from a vendor, sell on the auction house

        Vendors your faction can reach sell these at a fixed price. The auction price is what sellers ask, after the 5% cut, so a high price is no promise that it sells.

          Prices from AHledger, built from player scans. Prices are what sellers ask, not what sold.

          Recipes, skill levels and vendor prices come from the Forever game files (build ). Which vendors sell an item comes from MetaBot's Classic vendor lists, so a few may differ in Forever.

          Profit counts the 5% auction house cut. Listing deposits come back when an item sells and are left out.
