    Buy on the auction house, sell to a vendor

    These listings sit below what any vendor pays for the item. Buy the cheapest one and sell it to a vendor. The scan can be hours old, so check the price before you buy.

      Buy materials, craft, sell to a vendor

      A vendor pays more for these than their materials cost. Auction materials count at the price setting above, vendor goods at vendor price after your discount, and in-between materials like cured hides at whichever is cheaper, buying or crafting. Tap one for the full bill.

        Buy from a vendor, sell on the auction house

        Vendors sell these at a fixed price that your faction can reach. The auction price is what sellers ask, after the 5% cut. Few sales get recorded on small houses, so a high price is no promise that it sells.

          Prices from AHledger, built from player scans. Prices are what sellers ask, not what sold.
