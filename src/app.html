How the sim works

Model, numbers and sources

Questing: you fight one normal mob of the chosen level. Its health and damage come from the Forever client tables for that level; your armor and dodge cut the damage. Four ways to fight are compared: melee, melee with Earth Shock on cooldown, a Lightning Bolt pull before melee, and Lightning Bolt only. Each uses whichever weapon imbue you have that does the most damage, and Lightning Shield when it is on. If a fight would take you under 30% health, you cast Healing Wave mid-fight. After each kill you walk to the next mob, regenerating, then eat and drink at the same time for whatever you still need, using the best vendor food and water for your level. Healing Wave between pulls is used when it shortens the rest. Kills per hour is 3,600 divided by the seconds per kill. A style is marked unsafe if you run out of mana mid-fight or the mob out-damages Healing Wave.

Each fight is played out second by second, 40 times with different random rolls. The boss swings at the tank every 2 s; 30% of swings are avoided, 10% hit 50% harder. Every 15 s the whole group takes a hit. Your Shaman drops Healing Stream and Mana Spring once you have them, keeps Water Shield up when talented, uses Riptide on the tank, Chain Heal when three or more people are hurt, Healing Wave on the tank sized for the damage that lands during the cast, Nature's Swiftness or Lesser Healing Wave below 35% health, Mana Tide below 55% mana, and a potion when it fits. Healing that lands on full health counts as overheal. "Most tank damage you can heal through" is found by searching for the highest damage where nobody dies in at least 90% of fights.

Healing over 5 minutes is a separate calculation used for upgrades: how much you can heal with 70% Healing Wave and 30% Chain Heal before mana runs out, then at the rate your regen allows. Damage numbers are expected values against one target: melee with your best weapon imbue, Stormstrike when talented, and Earth Shock on cooldown while mana lasts.

Every spell uses the highest rank you have at your level, with its level scaling, and talents are the first points of each build's leveling route. Below 40 there is no Chain Heal, so healing over 5 minutes uses Healing Wave alone. Default fight numbers scale with an elite of your level: change them freely.

Not modelled: healing threat and pulling aggro, cast pushback (Healing Focus), Ancestral Healing's armor, movement, dispels, item procs and on-use trinkets, totem placement outside range, Searing Totem, adds and running mobs while questing, and health regeneration outside eating.

    Read a combat log

    Type /combatlog in game before a dungeon, and again after. The file is WoWCombatLog.txt in the Logs folder of your WoW Forever install. Turn on Advanced Combat Logging in the game's network settings so mana shows up. The file is read here in your browser and never uploaded.
