## Dungeon XP and quests

Dungeon mob XP is cut on every run, including the first. Dungeon quests pay about double Classic and can only be done once, so the first clear with all quests is where the XP is. Aggrend (Josh Greenfield) says pure dungeon grinding will stay "very close to optimal questing at best".

| Date       | Change                                                                                                                                   |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nov 2      | Launch notes: dungeon XP unchanged                                                                                                       |
| Oct 8      | Kill XP up about 20%. Party XP now based on your own level. If someone in the party is vastly higher level than the mob, nobody gets XP. |
| Oct 1      | Extra quest XP on top of Classic cut by 50%                                                                                              |
| Beta start | Mob XP cut (Method: about 70% below Classic; Blizzard only said "way down"), quest XP boosted                                            |

Examples: Deviate Hides (Wailing Caverns) 1,600 XP in Classic, 3,100 in Forever. Deadmines quests 8,300 in Classic, 18,450 in Forever (includes a new quest).

Group XP: in a full five-man group each player gets 28% of a solo kill's XP (split five ways, times 1.4). Quest XP is not split. Method's advice: quest through a zone, end with its dungeon, collect and share quests before going in.

Alliance quest pickups:

- Ruins of Lordaeron: all four Alliance quests start inside.

- Deadmines: Wilder Thistlenettle and Shoni in Stormwind's Dwarven District; Gryan Stoutmantle, Scout Riell and Alba Fairmoon at Sentinel Hill. VanCleef's Unsent Letter goes to Baros Alexston in Stormwind.

- Drowned City: Magics of the Drowned City, Take Two With Water (givers not found).

## Restoration Shaman

On Alliance, only Dwarves can be Shamans. Shamans have no Bloodlust in Forever.

Key changes:

- Riptide is the 31-point Resto talent. Since Nov 3 it has no upfront heal, only a heal over time, a 12-second cooldown and about 40% lower mana cost. It makes Chain Heal 25% stronger on the Riptide target. (Some guides still describe the old upfront heal.)

- Water Shield is the 11-point talent. Since Oct 16 it triggers on any spell crit. Beta guide: 3 orbs, each returns 2% of max mana.

- Mana Tide is the 16-point talent. Drop it early in a fight.

- Call of the Elements (level 20) drops up to four totems at once. Call of the Spirits at 30, Call of the Ancestors at 40 (Warcraft Tavern says Ancestors at 30).

- Totems: 1.0-second global cooldown, long totems last 5 minutes. Totemic Call (renamed Nov 3) moves your totems to you, 30-second cooldown. Totemic Recall destroys them and refunds 25% mana.

Group totems: since Oct 16, Grace of Air, Strength of Earth, Windfury and Flametongue reach the whole raid, and Grace of Air can run alongside Windfury or Flametongue. Resistance totems also reach the whole raid.

Leveling as a group healer: Warcraft Tavern (beta) says early dungeons don't need Resto talents and suggests healing in another spec until dual spec at 40. Routine: Water Shield up, Healing Stream or Mana Spring down, Healing Wave as main heal, Lesser Healing Wave for emergencies. Legacy points can unlock talents up to 5 levels earlier.

Key talents (beta, level 30): Mindfulness, Water Shield, Mana Tide, Ancestral Healing, Tidal Mastery, Nature's Swiftness, Riptide. No level-60 build is published yet.

Stat priority (beta): Healing Power, Spirit, Intellect, Crit, Haste.

Professions (Warcraft Tavern, beta): Leatherworking for spell power leveling gear, Engineering for trinkets, Enchanting as an option. Camp buffs for a healer: Alchemy (mana regen), Herbalism (Intellect), Tailoring (Spirit).

Dwarf racials in Forever: Stoneform (10% less physical damage for 8 seconds, removes and gives immunity to bleeds, poisons and diseases, 3-minute cooldown), Artisan's Precision (+1% hit with spells and attacks), Find Treasure (works alongside other tracking, chance of extra chest items), Big Game Hunter (+5% damage to beasts). Mace and Gun Specialization and Frost Resistance are gone.

Totem quests (vanilla levels, not confirmed by Blizzard for Forever): Earth at 4 (Teo Hammerstorm, Anvilmar), Fire at 10 (Bruegs Kindleborn, Dun Morogh, continues in Loch Modan), Water at 20 (Norric Lochthane, Loch Modan, long trip), Air at 30 (Shaman trainer, leads to Arathi; rewards the Enhancement weapon Rage of the Storm).

## Enhancement and dual spec

Dual spec unlocks at level 40 from the class trainer. Cost not announced (Season of Discovery charged 50g; not confirmed for Forever).

Enhancement uses two-handers. Blizzard made Two-Handed Axes and Maces baseline; no source confirms dual wield, and one guide found no Dual Wield talent in the launch tree.

Stats: Blizzard said on Nov 6 that Intellect and Spell Power currently beat Strength and Attack Power, and hotfixes on Nov 10 should make Strength the top stat. Collect Strength and Attack Power gear while leveling.

Key talents (Nov 3 notes): Stormstrike (150% normalized weapon damage, 6-second cooldown), Mental Dexterity (attack power equal to 50/100/150% of Intellect), Flurry (up to 30% attack speed), Maelstrom Weapon (proc chance doubled, Lightning Bolt only). Rage of the Farseer is a self-only attack-speed cooldown.

Tanking: Blizzard added some tools (Spirit Weapons, Improved Stormstrike, Anticipation) but no taunt. Warcraft Tavern doubts it is enough for endgame.

Gear overlap with Resto: Intellect counts for both (via Mental Dexterity), crit is merged across melee and spells, and bonus healing gives a third as much bonus damage. Weapons are always separate, and Strength is useless for Resto.

## Protection Paladin alt

Alliance Paladins: Human or Dwarf (from Warcraft Tavern's demo access, not Blizzard). A Dwarf Paladin could share the Shaman's surname.

- Taunt: Judgement with Seal of Fury active taunts within 10 yards. The Nov 3 notes cut "Judgement of Fury" from 4 to 3 seconds (that this is the taunt debuff is an inference).

- Judgement no longer consumes your seal. Judgement debuffs last 40 seconds.

- Righteous Fury trains at 10: +33% threat, +90% threat from Holy damage.

- Holy Strike: trained at 6 (beta), 10-second base cooldown. New row-7 talent Shieldsworn cuts its cooldown by 4 seconds, makes it hit with your shield and lets you block spells for 6 seconds.

- Nov 3: Improved Seal of Fury removed; Swift Judgement now instantly unleashes your seal and no longer resets Judgement (older guides describe the reset); Holy Shield moved to row 5; Blessing of Salvation and Blessing of Light removed.

- Consecration and Blessing of Kings train at 20. Guides suggest fast one-handers while leveling.

- Stat priority (Oct 26 guide): Hit to 3%, then Defense/Block, Stamina/Armor, Spell Power, Strength.

- Guides call it a strong AoE-threat tank limited by mana. No ranking after the Nov 3 changes yet.

- Paladins get their mount from a class quest.

## Systems

**Mounts** (guide-site beta prices; Blizzard confirmed the mount is now included in riding training but published no prices):

| Tier                            | Level | Cost                     | Speed |
|---------------------------------|-------|--------------------------|-------|
| Apprentice Riding + first mount | 40    | 100g (extra colours 20g) | 60%   |
| Journeyman Riding               | 60    | 1,000g                   | 100%  |
| Swift mount                     | 60    | 200g                     | 100%  |

Honored with your home faction gives 10% off. Dwarf riding trainer: Ultham Ironhorn, Amberstill Ranch.

**Rested XP:** built in inns, capitals and with the Cozy Sleeping Bag. 5% of a level per 8 hours, cap 150% (about 10 days to fill). Doubles kill XP only, not quest XP. Well Fed gives +5% kill XP. Quests more than 5 levels below you lose 10-20% XP per extra level. A week logged off in an inn builds about 105% of a level in rested XP (5% per 8 hours), close to the 150% cap.

**Camping:** the Cooking Fire is now a Basic Campfire that creates a campsite with profession workspaces and one-hour buffs (sit by it about a minute). A basic fire holds 3 objects, one per player; cooks can learn fires for 5 or 10 (required Cooking skill conflicts between guides). Each profession has three camp objects, the first at skill 20. Engineering objects provide vendors (Reagent Bot) and repairs (Repair Bot). Camp buffs don't stack with class buffs. Storage at camp: unconfirmed.

**Professions:** over 600 new recipes in total across professions (not per profession, as PC Gamer wrote). Advanced Blueprint recipes for camp objects drop from dungeon bosses. Max skill is 300, no new profession, gathering bags get their own slot. Crafted food can give a small XP bonus.

**Legacy:** 65 Legacy Challenges, 1 point each, shared across the account (except Hardcore). Unlocks with your first point, usually at level 25. Points come from leveling each class to 25, 45 and 60, and from profession milestones. Each character spends up to 16 points across three trees (Professions, Adventure, Resourcefulness). Perks include Reagent Economy (no vendor reagents for class abilities, free tier-1 camp objects), Permanence (longer buffs), Talented (talents earlier), Well Rested, Frequent Flier, Field Guide. Rewards at 15, 25, 40 and 55 points: an air rifle toy, then the Spectral Bear pet, tabard and mount. A Paladin alt adds to the shared pool.

**Transmog:** optional. Alliance transmogrifier is Fyrenz Vishonar in Stormwind's Mage Quarter. Uncommon and rare BoP dungeon drops give the look to every eligible looter (armor type rules apply); epic raid drops only to the winner. Appearances and outfit slots are account-wide; 2 free outfits.

**Characters:** no permanent 3-character limit (that was only during name reservation).

- **Gathering:** herbs and leather sell above the EU median here, copper below. Briarthorn 3s97 vs 1s50 EU, Medium Leather 2s45 vs 1s62, Heavy Leather 9s vs 6s49, Copper Ore 39c vs 71c ([Timesaver, EU medians Nov 7](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-auction-house-prices-week-1)). With open slots, Skinning plus Herbalism fits these prices best.

- **Sell the same day:** Scarce mats fell about 60% in a week here. EU-wide, level 30-45 mats fell 50-67% from launch week as more players reached those zones.

- **Waylaid Crates:** unfilled crates aren't soulbound and sell. Apprentice crate 7-day medians: Ingots 54s, Fabrics 50s, Textiles 36s, Ore 28s, Curiosities 24s ([AHledger deals](https://ahledger.com/deals/forever/rppvp/alliance/eu)).

- **Upgrading:** Copper Ore 39c smelts into a 38c bar, Tin Ore 3s80 into a 3s20 bar. Bronze clears about 60c a smelt from bought bars and roughly zero from own ore. One Copper Bar and one Tin Bar make two Bronze Bars at Mining 65 ([recipe](https://seemeta.com/en/wow-forever/professions/mining)).

- **Leveling crafts are flooded:** most listed this week are Embossed Leather Gloves (243), Lesser Magic Wand (239), Red Linen Bag (180) and Linen Bag (177).

- **Cross-house arbitrage:** no realms, so no cross-realm flips. Cross-faction trade through a neutral auction house is the only arbitrage left, at a 15% cut instead of 5%.

- **Faction gaps:** on Nov 8 the Horde RP-PvP EU house had Copper Bar at 1s87 and Wool at 1s74, against our 38c and 98c ([AHledger Horde](https://ahledger.com/markets/forever/rppvp/horde/eu)). Their Copper Bar is up about 300% on the week, so the gap may not last.

- **One way only:** we're faction-locked, so we can only post at a neutral auctioneer and wait for Horde buyers. Neutral houses: Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Everlook, and reportedly Auctioneer Quickcoin at the Commerce Authority camp in western Redridge ([Conquest Capped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-azeroth-commerce-authority-reputation/)).

- **Gear deals:** items 50-70% under median show up (Golden Rod 95s vs 2g 79s), but sales per day are unknown. EU single-item buyouts failed with "item not found" from Nov 6.

- **AH rules:** 5% cut at the faction house, 15% at neutral ones. The deposit depends on vendor price and duration (12, 24 or 48 hours) and is lost if the listing expires or is cancelled ([AHledger rules](https://ahledger.com/wow-forever/auction-house)).

- **Posting:** the auxForever author says the newest listing at a price sells first, so match the lowest price instead of undercutting ([auxForever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auxforever)).

- **Tool:** the [AHledger addon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ahledger) adds median, 7-day change and listed count to tooltips, plus crafting margins. Its scans feed the site's page for our house.

**Craft tool:** [Forever Craft Profits](https://chraltro.github.io/wowsim/crafts/) (on the wowsim site; it loads the latest AHledger scan when it opens) ranks every recipe by profit on our house, with vendor reagents at vendor price. Sale prices more than 2× other EU houses are cut to 2×, and one-off listings no other house backs up are hidden. Its Vendor flips tab lists AH listings below vendor price and vendor items that sell above vendor price on the AH. On the Nov 7 scan that meant 5 listings below vendor price (best +1s 33c) and about 50 vendor items above it, mostly vendor recipes and manuals with 1 to 7 listed.

**Craft and vendor (Nov 9):** buy materials, craft, sell the result to a vendor. A player tip: Light Leather Pants (Leatherworking 80) need 10 Light Leather, 1 Cured Light Hide (Light Hide plus Salt) and 1 Fine Thread, and vendor for 5s 99c. On our house's Nov 7 prices that bill is 9s 55c at the cheapest listings (Light Hide 4s 05c, Light Leather 40c), a 3s 56c loss. It works only with Light Leather near 30c and Light Hide under about 1s 50c. The tip's Fine Thread at 90c and Salt at 45c are the 10% Honored discount off vendor prices of 1s and 50c. Best craft-and-vendor on our house: Lean Venison, +82c per craft (Recipe: Lean Venison, 8 Stag Meat listed). The tool's Vendor flips tab lists these and has a vendor discount setting.

**Expected profit (Nov 10):** the craft tool now ranks by what a craft brings on average. Sell speed is AHledger's estimate from consecutive scans (units sold a day against units listed). Our house has no estimate yet, so it borrows the median of the EU houses that do. The expectation posts for 48 hours up to three times and vendors leftovers. On our house, 166 recipes at skill 150 or below profit on paper and 140 are expected to.

**Deposits:** a faction-house listing costs 15% of the item's vendor price per 12 hours, 60% for 48 hours, back on sale and lost on expiry (AHledger, Classic Era rules that Forever keeps). Items whose auction price sits near their vendor price, like Linen Bags, can be better vendored.

**Item type does not predict what sells:** across EU houses, 12% of crafted green armor and 24% of crafted consumables barely sell (stock takes over 14 days to clear), while 18% and 17% clear in under a day.

**Recipes worth buying (Nov 10):** the craft tool's Recipes tab ranks recipe items by what a first batch of crafts is expected to make after paying for the recipe. On our house 23 recipes for sale pay for themselves in one batch. Pattern: Barbaric Bracers (20s, vendor, limited stock) and Schematic: Minor Recombobulator (15s, vendor) pay back in one craft, but only 4 and 10 of the results are listed, so that is one or two crafts at a time. Recipe: Elixir of Lesser Agility (35s) and Recipe: Elixir of Minor Agility (20s) pay back in four crafts and sell in volume. Merchant's Favor costs about 1s 70c a favor through Journeyman Herbs crates, so a 30-favor recipe is about 51s. No favor recipe pays back in one batch.

**Leveling a profession (Nov 10):** the tool's Leveling tab gives the cheapest route between two skills at our prices, with a shopping list. From 1 to 150, selling what you make is expected to cover the materials in every crafting profession but First Aid, which costs 19s. Materials to put up: Tailoring 11g 82s, Leatherworking 15g 71s, Blacksmithing 14g 46s, Alchemy 11g 18s, Engineering 5g 56s, Cooking 90s. From 150 to 225 it costs money: Engineering about 3g, Alchemy 5g, Leatherworking 9g, Cooking 14g, Tailoring 30g, Blacksmithing 130g. Our house lists too little Silk Cloth, Bolt of Mageweave and Mithril Bar for those runs in one go. Trainer recipes stop giving points at 255 in Tailoring and 250 in Leatherworking.

**Trainer levels (Nov 10):** Forever moved about half of Classic's recipes down the skill range (576 of the 1,092 both games share), each by a fixed amount, most often 25 points. Yellow and grey moved together in 1,085 of the 1,092. The client does not say when a trainer teaches a recipe. Classic's level moved by the same amount matches the client's own requirement for 644 of 669 recipes taught by an item, so the tool uses that, with Classic's trainer fees.

**Post it or vendor it (Nov 10):** the tool's Flips search now checks any item you type. It posts one unit just under the cheapest listing and compares the expected sale (5% cut, chance of selling in 48 hours, a deposit of 60% of the vendor price lost on each expired listing, up to three tries) with the vendor price. On the 10 Nov scan of our house, Patterned Bronze Bracers were expected to bring 6s 48c posted against 5s 7c from a vendor, and a Militant Shortsword was better vendored at 19s 16c than posted at an expected 17s 22c. Our house has no 7-day or 30-day history of its own yet, because both figures were identical on all 2,158 items, so buying cheap listings to relist is not built. A refresh script rebuilds the tool's data from fresh scans of all 24 houses in about a minute and a half.

**Cost of Merchant's Favor (Nov 10 scan):** buying an empty Waylaid Crate and its cheapest fill costs about 1s 70c to 3s a favor on our house. Cheapest was Journeyman Herbs: crate 3s 96c plus 10 Bruiseweed for 13s, for 10 favor. White Apprentice crates give 5 favor, green Apprentice and white Journeyman 10, green Journeyman 20 ([wow.gg](https://wow.gg/guides/merchants-favor)). Expert and Artisan amounts are not confirmed. The tool's Market tab keeps this current.

## RP-PvP rules and names

- RP-PvP was added for launch. Blizzard's definition: Roleplay naming and behavior rules plus constant risk of attack from the other faction. Method reads it as auto-flagged everywhere except sanctuaries and your own faction's areas.

- PvP rulesets lock you to one faction; Method says that applies to RP-PvP too. Characters can't change ruleset. You can only group with players on the same ruleset; battlegrounds mix rulesets.

- Every character has a first name and a secondary name. The pair must be unique per region. Each part is 2 to 12 letters, no apostrophes or spaces. Family names are allowed, so the group can share a surname. You can hide your own secondary name in the world (Options \> Nameplates), but it still shows in chat.

- Names must be lore-friendly. Non-fantasy names, famous people, brands and multi-word names can be flagged, leading to a forced rename and possible account action. Blizzard hasn't published how enforcement works.

- Launch week: Horde creation was reportedly locked on PvP and RP-PvP due to population imbalance; Alliance was not affected (unofficial source).

## Design pillars and what differs from vanilla

Blizzard says every change has to pass four pillars. The aim is to build on vanilla while staying true to its "core design, pacing, and tone" ("WoW as you remember it, not as it was").

| Pillar                                 | Meaning                                                                                  | Examples                                                |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Approachable and familiar              | Simple classes, methodical combat, no add-ons needed, leveling meant to take a long time | No flying, no level scaling                             |
| Journey before destination             | Leveling matters as much as endgame; gaps in the leveling path filled                    | Dungeon mob XP cut; new dungeons for empty level ranges |
| Protect and nurture social experiences | No realms; the game tracks who you've met so you see the same people                     | No summoning stones; shared campfires                   |
| World as the main character            | You're an ordinary soldier, not the hero; grounded local quests                          | Set before Molten Core; Arthas and Illidan still alive  |

Main differences from vanilla:

- World: new zones Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Zephras Isle (Skyborne start), Dalaran in Alterac, over 1,000 new quests, 9 new dungeons, new battleground Darkspear Islands. Level cap stays 60 for good.

- Races and classes: Skyborne (paid pack; Alliance High Order can be Mage, Horde Windshapers can be Shaman). New combos: Human Hunter, Orc Mage, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Undead Paladin, Troll Warlock. Racials reworked.

- Abilities and talents: 136 new class spells, 245 retuned; Season of Discovery runes like Mutilate, Penance and Lava Burst are trained abilities; 381 of 432 talents changed; every one of the 27 trees meant to be viable.

- Items: 3,916 new, 2,055 reworked. Hit and crit merged across melee and spells; caster weapons get spell damage; unique dungeon boss items are blue; chase items on every raid boss; situational effects by environment and enemy type.

- No WoW Token, no paid boosts. Uses the modern addon system, so Classic addons may not work. Old/HD model toggle.
