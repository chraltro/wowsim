# What each class brings

The rest of the group: Warrior, Druid, Warlock, Mage and Hunter.

On Nov 3 Blizzard raised damage for every class and raised health on higher-level mobs. Blessing of Salvation is gone, so tank threat went up: Defensive Stance from 30% to 80%, Bear Form from 50% to 115%. Big tuning is on hold until raids open. Training every ability 1-60 costs about 206g.

Hotfixes Nov 6-10 ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)):

- Warrior: Arms' Sweeping Strikes keeps its charges when no second target is hit.

- Druid: Feral Charge (Cat) puts you in combat at once, Natural Instinct applies to Lacerate and Pounce, Idol of Ferocity no longer discounts Mangle. Restoration's Nature's Swiftness also makes Wild Growth instant.

- Warlock: Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus and Incubus have about 15% more base health, and the first four ranks of the Imp's Firebolt hit harder. Affliction's Wrack deals 30% less damage and now makes Siphon Life, Corruption and Bane of Agony tick twice as fast while channelled; Soul Siphon counts only those three (4/8/12% each, up to 36%). Bane of Havoc triggers the global cooldown. Incinerate is Destruction only.

- Mage: Arcane Concentration's chance is split across the cast and each bolt (was 10% on each). Arcane Blast lasts 18 seconds. Flamestrike's ground ticks no longer eat Clearcasting.

- Hunter: pet health is 115% of normal (it was 150% by mistake). Arcane Shot costs 11% of base mana at every rank. Beast Mastery's Hawk hits about 80% harder.

- World: Blizzard lowered the population in crowded zones on Nov 9, more so on the PvP ruleset.
