# Systems

Mounts, rested XP, camping, professions, Legacy, transmog, RP-PvP rules, and what Forever changed from vanilla.

## Systems

**Mounts** (guide-site beta prices; Blizzard confirmed the mount is now included in riding training but published no prices):

| Tier                            | Level | Cost                     | Speed |
|---------------------------------|-------|--------------------------|-------|
| Apprentice Riding + first mount | 40    | 100g (extra colours 20g) | 60%   |
| Journeyman Riding               | 60    | 1,000g                   | 100%  |
| Swift mount                     | 60    | 200g                     | 100%  |

Honored with your home faction gives 10% off. Dwarf riding trainer: Ultham Ironhorn, Amberstill Ranch. Since Nov 10 all racial vendor mounts are bind-on-pickup.

**Rested XP:** built in inns, capitals and with the Cozy Sleeping Bag. 5% of a level per 8 hours, cap 150% (about 10 days to fill). Doubles kill XP only, not quest XP. Well Fed gives +5% kill XP. Quests more than 5 levels below you lose 10-20% XP per extra level. A week logged off in an inn builds about 105% of a level in rested XP (5% per 8 hours), close to the 150% cap.

**Camping:** the Cooking Fire is now a Basic Campfire that creates a campsite with profession workspaces and one-hour buffs (sit by it about a minute). A basic fire holds 3 objects, one per player; cooks can learn fires for 5 or 10 (the Journeyman Campfire Kit needs Cooking 90 to place since Nov 10, was 140; the next tier's skill still conflicts between guides). Each profession has three camp objects, the first at skill 20. Engineering objects provide vendors (Reagent Bot) and repairs (Repair Bot). Camp buffs don't stack with class buffs. Storage at camp: unconfirmed.

**Professions:** over 600 new recipes in total across professions (not per profession, as PC Gamer wrote). Advanced Blueprint recipes for camp objects drop from dungeon bosses. Max skill is 300, no new profession, gathering bags get their own slot. Crafted food can give a small XP bonus. Hotfixes ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)): Leatherworking's Tanning Rack needs skill 125 (was 140) and its skill-ups dropped by 15 (Nov 6). Coarse Grinding Stone gives Blacksmithing points up to 90 (was 75). Enchant Boots and Enchant Bracer - Lesser Spirit now give Spirit (were Resist All), and the Arcane Salvager needs the Runed Golden Rod. Engineering's Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow are no longer bind-on-pickup. Fishing Racks sometimes gave no lures; partly fixed, still being looked at (all Nov 10).

**Legacy:** 65 Legacy Challenges, 1 point each, shared across the account (except Hardcore). Unlocks with your first point, usually at level 25. Points come from leveling each class to 25, 45 and 60, and from profession milestones. Each character spends up to 16 points across three trees (Professions, Adventure, Resourcefulness). Perks include Reagent Economy (no vendor reagents for class abilities, free tier-1 camp objects), Permanence (longer buffs), Talented (talents earlier), Well Rested, Frequent Flier, Field Guide. Rewards at 15, 25, 40 and 55 points: an air rifle toy, then the Spectral Bear pet, tabard and mount. A Paladin alt adds to the shared pool.

**Transmog:** optional. Alliance transmogrifier is Fyrenz Vishonar in Stormwind's Mage Quarter. Uncommon and rare BoP dungeon drops give the look to every eligible looter (armor type rules apply); epic raid drops only to the winner. Appearances and outfit slots are account-wide; 2 free outfits.

**Characters:** no permanent 3-character limit (that was only during name reservation).

## RP-PvP rules and names

- RP-PvP was added for launch. Blizzard's definition: Roleplay naming and behavior rules plus constant risk of attack from the other faction. Method reads it as auto-flagged everywhere except sanctuaries and your own faction's areas.

- PvP rulesets lock you to one faction; Method says that applies to RP-PvP too. Characters can't change ruleset. You can only group with players on the same ruleset; battlegrounds mix rulesets.

- Every character has a first name and a secondary name. The pair must be unique per region. Each part is 2 to 12 letters, no apostrophes or spaces. Family names are allowed, so the group can share a surname. You can hide your own secondary name in the world (Options \> Nameplates), but it still shows in chat.

- Names must be lore-friendly. Non-fantasy names, famous people, brands and multi-word names can be flagged, leading to a forced rename and possible account action. Blizzard hasn't published how enforcement works.

- Launch week: Horde creation was reportedly locked on PvP and RP-PvP due to population imbalance; Alliance was not affected (unofficial source).

## Design pillars and what differs from vanilla

Blizzard says every change has to pass four pillars. The aim is to build on vanilla while staying true to its "core design, pacing, and tone" ("WoW as you remember it, not as it was").

| Pillar                                 | Meaning                                                                                  | Examples                                                |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Approachable and familiar              | Simple classes, methodical combat, no add-ons needed, leveling meant to take a long time | No flying, no level scaling                             |
| Journey before destination             | Leveling matters as much as endgame; gaps in the leveling path filled                    | Dungeon mob XP cut; new dungeons for empty level ranges |
| Protect and nurture social experiences | No realms; the game tracks who you've met so you see the same people                     | No summoning stones; shared campfires                   |
| World as the main character            | You're an ordinary soldier, not the hero; grounded local quests                          | Set before Molten Core; Arthas and Illidan still alive  |

Main differences from vanilla:

- World: new zones Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas, Zephras Isle (Skyborne start), Dalaran in Alterac, over 1,000 new quests, 9 new dungeons, new battleground Darkspear Islands. Level cap stays 60 for good.

- Races and classes: Skyborne (paid pack; Alliance High Order can be Mage, Horde Windshapers can be Shaman). New combos: Human Hunter, Orc Mage, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Undead Paladin, Troll Warlock. Racials reworked.

- Abilities and talents: 136 new class spells, 245 retuned; Season of Discovery runes like Mutilate, Penance and Lava Burst are trained abilities; 381 of 432 talents changed; every one of the 27 trees meant to be viable.

- Items: 3,916 new, 2,055 reworked. Hit and crit merged across melee and spells; caster weapons get spell damage; unique dungeon boss items are blue; chase items on every raid boss; situational effects by environment and enemy type.

- No WoW Token, no paid boosts. Uses the modern addon system, so Classic addons may not work. Old/HD model toggle.
