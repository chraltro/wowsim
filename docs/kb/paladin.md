# Protection Paladin alt

Notes for the planned tank alt.

Alliance Paladins: Human or Dwarf (from Warcraft Tavern's demo access, not Blizzard). A Dwarf Paladin could share the Shaman's surname.

- Taunt: Judgement with Seal of Fury active taunts within 10 yards. The Nov 3 notes cut "Judgement of Fury" from 4 to 3 seconds (that this is the taunt debuff is an inference).

- Judgement no longer consumes your seal. Judgement debuffs last 40 seconds.

- Righteous Fury trains at 10: +33% threat, +90% threat from Holy damage.

- Holy Strike: trained at 6 (beta), 10-second base cooldown. New row-7 talent Shieldsworn cuts its cooldown by 4 seconds, makes it hit with your shield and lets you block spells for 6 seconds.

- Nov 3: Improved Seal of Fury removed; Swift Judgement now instantly unleashes your seal and no longer resets Judgement (older guides describe the reset); Holy Shield moved to row 5; Blessing of Salvation and Blessing of Light removed.

- Consecration and Blessing of Kings train at 20. Guides suggest fast one-handers while leveling.

- Stat priority (Oct 26 guide): Hit to 3%, then Defense/Block, Stamina/Armor, Spell Power, Strength.

- Guides call it a strong AoE-threat tank limited by mana. No ranking after the Nov 3 changes yet.

- Paladins get their mount from a class quest.

- Hotfixes Nov 9-10: Consecration keeps its increased damage when targets inside it despawn, and Redoubt and Seals show correctly in the Cooldown Manager ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)).
