# Gold making

What sells on our auction house, flips, crafting, and what a profession costs to level.

## The house on Nov 12

AHledger's scan of our house from Nov 12, 16:00 UTC, set against the other EU houses scanned the same day. Sold a day is AHledger's estimate from consecutive scans and leans low. This replaces the Nov 8 picture below where the two disagree.

About 6,300g changes hands a day on our house: trade goods 2,100g, armor 1,225g, recipes 1,010g, consumables 625g, weapons 500g. It lists 2,819 different items against about 5,800 on PvP EU Alliance, so it is a thin market and a few sellers set each price.

Several goods from the level 10 to 30 zones cost far more here than on any other EU house. Where our price is many times the others, plan on the 7-day median and treat today's price as a bonus.

Flat or falling here: Heavy Stone, Medium Leather, Light Leather, Linen, Copper, Tin, Silk, Pristine Hide (9s, was 36s).

- **Savory Whimsyfin Delight** is a new cosmetic food: it turns you into an Orc Pirate or Orc Ninja for an hour ([Method](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/savory-whimsyfin-delight-and-savory-deviate-delight-how-to-turn-into-a-pirate-or-ninja-in-wow-forever); the beta's Murloc and Ogre forms were placeholders). Whimsyfin comes only from fishing schools in Redridge, mostly by the murloc village at the small lake in the east. The recipe drops from any Redridge mob and can be traded (5g on our house, one listed, 7-day median 7g 79c). In the game data it is Cooking 85 and one Whimsyfin plus one Mild Spices makes one dish. One guide says two; raw Whimsyfin hardly sells here (3.5 a day at 20s), which fits one. Buying the fish to cook loses money, so the profit is in fishing it. Savory Deviate Delight does not sell on our house (15s, none sold).
- **Swiftthistle** comes with 39% of Briarthorn gathers and 20% of Mageroyal gathers, 1.5 on average each time ([timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/tools/wow-forever/professions/herbalism), from Wowhead's Forever gathering counts). That is about 6s of Swiftthistle per Briarthorn node and 3s per Mageroyal node at our price. Alliance zones: Westfall, Darkshore, Duskwood, Ashenvale. The price is as high on PvP EU Alliance, so this one is not a local spike.
- **Stranglekelp** gives 2 per node on average (same source).
- **Frilled Lichen** (Scarce, makes Cerulean Dye, which 131 recipes use) is a bonus from ordinary herb nodes at 0.2% to 1.1% a gather (same source). A beta article gave 4% to 20% ([Gamewave](https://gamewave.fr/world-of-warcraft-forever/wow-forever-lichen-a-volants-comment-en-obtenir/)); the live counts are the ones to trust. Its price is the same on every EU house.
- **Pristine Leather** has no published drop rate. On the three largest EU houses it sells at 6% to 7.5% of the combined Light and Medium Leather volume, which is the only hint. It goes into 126 recipes, none of whose products sell on our house, so the buyers are people leveling professions.
- **Small Furry Paw** is a Darkmoon Faire turn-in (five for a ticket). The Faire is in Elwynn from Nov 9 to Nov 15 and returns Dec 7 in Mulgore ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/the-darkmoon-faire-is-now-open-in-wow-forever/)); another site gives Nov 16 as the last day. Expect the price to fall back when it leaves.
- **Blueprints** from dungeon bosses sell: Trapper's Workbench 15g, Tanning Rack 10g, Arcane Salvager 9g, Rock Garden 8g 50c, Journeyman Campfire 6g 50c, Fermenter 6g, Field Guide 5g, each about 5 sold a day.
- **Vendor to auction house** (expected profit each after the cut and failed listings): Cat Carrier (White Kitten) 60s to 1g 99c, +1g 16c, 3 sold a day, one in stock (Lil Timmy, who wanders Stormwind; [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/dont-miss-these-cheap-pets-for-60-silver-in-wow-forever/) confirms him for Forever, respawn unknown). Manual: Mageweave Bandage 50s to 1g 25c, +57s, 9 a day. Expert Cookbook 1g to 1g 45c, +33s, 31 a day. Pattern: Red Woolen Bag 5s to 30s, 7 a day, limited stock. Recipe: Beer Basted Boar Ribs 2s 40c to 18s, 10 a day, limited. Recipe: Rage Potion 1s to 15s, 12 a day, limited. Recipe: Slitherskin Mackerel 40c to 7s, 21 a day. Together that is about 24g a day for everyone doing it. Vendor locations for all but the kitten are not checked for Forever.
- **Crafting** pools stay small. The largest by expected profit times daily sales: White Bandit Mask (Tailoring 180), Cured Heavy Hide (Leatherworking 125), Shadow Protection Potion (Alchemy 135, 42 sold a day with one listed), Red Woolen Bag (Tailoring 90, costs 18s, sells 48s, 35 a day), Bolt of Woolen Cloth, Bronze Tube (Engineering 105). Each is single-digit gold a day for the whole house.
- **Waylaid Crates have fallen:** Apprentice Ingots 38s, Journeyman Fabrics 25s, most others 1s to 6s.
- **The other faction on our realm** prices these very differently (Horde RP-PvP EU: Pristine Leather 10s, Spider's Silk 7s 60c, Small Furry Paw 2s 90c; Silver Ore 18s, Jade 30s, Grave Moss 19s 60c against 8s 90c, 10s and 3s 70c here). Only a neutral auction house connects the two, at a 15% cut, and there is no price data for neutral houses.
- **Coyote Meat** sells for 6s to 7s here and across the EU, 166 a day against 56 listed. Cooks level on Coyote Steak (Cooking 50) on the way to the Journeyman Campfire Kit. Coyotes (level 10 to 11) and Coyote Packleaders (11 to 12) in Westfall drop it about half the time ([metabot.gg](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/items/coyote-meat), Classic drop data). A route: Sentinel Hill, Saldean's Farm, Gold Coast Quarry, Jangolode Mine ([RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/world-of-warcraft/forever/professions/cooking-leveling/)). Other low-zone meat is worth copper: boar, bear and spider meat sell for about 50c.
- **Where the cheap recipes are sold** (Classic vendor data unless noted, so check the stock in game): Pattern: Red Woolen Bag from Rann Flamespinner in Thelsamar, Amy Davenport in Lakeshire and Jennabink Powerseam in Menethil, one in stock ([Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Pattern:_Red_Woolen_Bag)). Recipe: Beer Basted Boar Ribs (2s 40c) from Kendor Kabonka, upstairs in the Pig and Whistle in Stormwind, listed on his [Forever page](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/npc/kendor-kabonka). The bandage manuals (Arathi in Classic) and the Expert Cookbook (Ashenvale in Classic) need a higher level.
- **Do first-tier Scarce materials stop dropping after level 25?** Beta testers concluded on Oct 11 that Pristine Leather needs a character of level 25 or lower skinning a non-grey beast of level 10 to 25: a level 22 found it, a level 30 found none in hundreds of skins, and Frilled Lichen stopped for one player at 26 after about 150 more gathers ([expcarry](https://expcarry.com/pristine-leather-farming-wow-forever), [EU forum](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/634869)). Blizzard has not confirmed it and it is beta data. Against it: the big EU houses still sell Pristine Leather at 5s to 8s, a little down on the week. One beta player had about 15 Pristine Leather by Skinning 152, which fits the 6% to 7.5% volume hint above, so plan on about one skin in twenty.
- **What one kill sells for.** Every creature of level 9 to 25 in Alliance zones, valued at the cheapest listing on our house on Nov 12 (less the 5% cut, vendor price for what does not sell five a day). Drop chances are Classic's ([cmangos classic-db](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db)); levels, zones and spawn counts are Forever's (QuestieDB). The money is in cooking meat, and a few animals carry it. Everything else at level 9 to 14 (Defias, gnolls, troggs, kobolds, bears, wolves, spiders, murlocs) comes to 0.7s to 1.0s a kill with its linen and coin.

  - Level 14 to 20: Moonstalker (Darkshore, 14 to 15) 12s while the Faire lifts Small Furry Paw (56% in the Classic table, which one Classic player's comment disputes), 2s to 4s at the paw's usual price. Goretusk 2.9s, Great Goretusk 3.3s. Wood Lurker (Loch Modan, 17 to 18) 2.9s on Poisoned Spider Fang (28% at 7s, 16 sold a day). - **A level 10 to 15 character** reaches three pools worth the walk: Pristine Leather, Swiftthistle through Briarthorn and Mageroyal, and Coyote Meat. - **Gathering:** herbs and leather sell above the EU median here, copper below. Briarthorn 3s97 vs 1s50 EU, Medium Leather 2s45 vs 1s62, Heavy Leather 9s vs 6s49, Copper Ore 39c vs 71c ([Timesaver, EU medians Nov 7](https://timesaver.gg/blog/wow-forever-auction-house-prices-week-1)). With open slots, Skinning plus Herbalism fits these prices best.

- **Sell the same day:** Scarce mats fell about 60% in a week here. EU-wide, level 30-45 mats fell 50-67% from launch week as more players reached those zones.

- **Waylaid Crates:** unfilled crates aren't soulbound and sell. Apprentice crate 7-day medians: Ingots 54s, Fabrics 50s, Textiles 36s, Ore 28s, Curiosities 24s ([AHledger deals](https://ahledger.com/deals/forever/rppvp/alliance/eu)).

- **Upgrading:** Copper Ore 39c smelts into a 38c bar, Tin Ore 3s80 into a 3s20 bar. Bronze clears about 60c a smelt from bought bars and roughly zero from own ore. One Copper Bar and one Tin Bar make two Bronze Bars at Mining 65 ([recipe](https://seemeta.com/en/wow-forever/professions/mining)).

- **Leveling crafts are flooded:** most listed this week are Embossed Leather Gloves (243), Lesser Magic Wand (239), Red Linen Bag (180) and Linen Bag (177).

- **Cross-house arbitrage:** no realms, so no cross-realm flips. Cross-faction trade through a neutral auction house is the only arbitrage left, at a 15% cut instead of 5%.

- **Faction gaps:** on Nov 8 the Horde RP-PvP EU house had Copper Bar at 1s87 and Wool at 1s74, against our 38c and 98c ([AHledger Horde](https://ahledger.com/markets/forever/rppvp/horde/eu)). Their Copper Bar is up about 300% on the week, so the gap may not last.

- **One way only:** we're faction-locked, so we can only post at a neutral auctioneer and wait for Horde buyers. Neutral houses: Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Everlook, and reportedly Auctioneer Quickcoin at the Commerce Authority camp in western Redridge ([Conquest Capped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-azeroth-commerce-authority-reputation/)).

- **Gear deals:** items 50-70% under median show up (Golden Rod 95s vs 2g 79s), but sales per day are unknown. EU single-item buyouts failed with "item not found" from Nov 6.

- **AH rules:** 5% cut at the faction house, 15% at neutral ones. The deposit depends on vendor price and duration (12, 24 or 48 hours) and is lost if the listing expires or is cancelled ([AHledger rules](https://ahledger.com/wow-forever/auction-house)).

- **Posting:** the auxForever author says the newest listing at a price sells first, so match the lowest price instead of undercutting ([auxForever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auxforever)).

- **Tool:** the [AHledger addon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ahledger) adds median, 7-day change and listed count to tooltips, plus crafting margins. Its scans feed the site's page for our house.

**Craft tool:** [Forever Craft Profits](https://chraltro.github.io/wowsim/crafts/) (on the wowsim site; it loads the latest AHledger scan when it opens) ranks every recipe by profit on our house, with vendor reagents at vendor price. Sale prices more than 2× other EU houses are cut to 2×, and one-off listings no other house backs up are hidden. Its Vendor flips tab lists AH listings below vendor price and vendor items that sell above vendor price on the AH. On the Nov 7 scan that meant 5 listings below vendor price (best +1s 33c) and about 50 vendor items above it, mostly vendor recipes and manuals with 1 to 7 listed.

**Craft and vendor (Nov 9):** buy materials, craft, sell the result to a vendor. A player tip: Light Leather Pants (Leatherworking 80) need 10 Light Leather, 1 Cured Light Hide (Light Hide plus Salt) and 1 Fine Thread, and vendor for 5s 99c. On our house's Nov 7 prices that bill is 9s 55c at the cheapest listings (Light Hide 4s 05c, Light Leather 40c), a 3s 56c loss. It works only with Light Leather near 30c and Light Hide under about 1s 50c. The tip's Fine Thread at 90c and Salt at 45c are the 10% Honored discount off vendor prices of 1s and 50c. Best craft-and-vendor on our house: Lean Venison, +82c per craft (Recipe: Lean Venison, 8 Stag Meat listed). The tool's Vendor flips tab lists these and has a vendor discount setting.

**Expected profit (Nov 10):** the craft tool now ranks by what a craft brings on average. Sell speed is AHledger's estimate from consecutive scans (units sold a day against units listed). Our house has no estimate yet, so it borrows the median of the EU houses that do. The expectation posts for 48 hours up to three times and vendors leftovers. On our house, 166 recipes at skill 150 or below profit on paper and 140 are expected to.

**Deposits:** a faction-house listing costs 15% of the item's vendor price per 12 hours, 60% for 48 hours, back on sale and lost on expiry (AHledger, Classic Era rules that Forever keeps). Since Nov 10 the smallest deposit is 1 copper (was 1 silver), so cheap items cost almost nothing to list ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)). Items whose auction price sits near their vendor price, like Linen Bags, can be better vendored.

**Vendor prices cut (Nov 10):** Blizzard lowered what vendors pay for some crafted armor from Leatherworking, Tailoring and Blacksmithing (bind-on-pickup pieces meant to be crafted once), cut Iron Grenades to 1s 25c (was 5s) and Minor Arcane Elixir to 5c (was 25c). Mild Spices sell for 1c since Nov 5. Blizzard did not list the armor pieces. The craft tool's vendor prices come from client build 1.60.1.70862, older than these changes, so a craft-and-vendor result for crafted armor may be too high until its data is rebuilt ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)).

**More gold and crates from play (Nov 9-10):** Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs now drop inside dungeons, and most quests of level 48 and up also pay gold.

**Item type does not predict what sells:** across EU houses, 12% of crafted green armor and 24% of crafted consumables barely sell (stock takes over 14 days to clear), while 18% and 17% clear in under a day.

**Recipes worth buying (Nov 10):** the craft tool's Recipes tab ranks recipe items by what a first batch of crafts is expected to make after paying for the recipe. On our house 23 recipes for sale pay for themselves in one batch. Pattern: Barbaric Bracers (20s, vendor, limited stock) and Schematic: Minor Recombobulator (15s, vendor) pay back in one craft, but only 4 and 10 of the results are listed, so that is one or two crafts at a time. Recipe: Elixir of Lesser Agility (35s) and Recipe: Elixir of Minor Agility (20s) pay back in four crafts and sell in volume. Merchant's Favor costs about 1s 70c a favor through Journeyman Herbs crates, so a 30-favor recipe is about 51s. No favor recipe pays back in one batch.

**Leveling a profession (Nov 10):** the tool's Leveling tab gives the cheapest route between two skills at our prices, with a shopping list. From 1 to 150, selling what you make is expected to cover the materials in every crafting profession but First Aid, which costs 19s. Materials to put up: Tailoring 11g 82s, Leatherworking 15g 71s, Blacksmithing 14g 46s, Alchemy 11g 18s, Engineering 5g 56s, Cooking 90s. From 150 to 225 it costs money: Engineering about 3g, Alchemy 5g, Leatherworking 9g, Cooking 14g, Tailoring 30g, Blacksmithing 130g. Our house lists too little Silk Cloth, Bolt of Mageweave and Mithril Bar for those runs in one go. Trainer recipes stop giving points at 255 in Tailoring and 250 in Leatherworking.

**Trainer levels (Nov 10):** Forever moved about half of Classic's recipes down the skill range (576 of the 1,092 both games share), each by a fixed amount, most often 25 points. Yellow and grey moved together in 1,085 of the 1,092. The client does not say when a trainer teaches a recipe. Classic's level moved by the same amount matches the client's own requirement for 644 of 669 recipes taught by an item, so the tool uses that, with Classic's trainer fees.

**Post it or vendor it (Nov 10):** the tool's Flips search now checks any item you type. It posts one unit just under the cheapest listing and compares the expected sale (5% cut, chance of selling in 48 hours, a deposit of 60% of the vendor price lost on each expired listing, up to three tries) with the vendor price. On the 10 Nov scan of our house, Patterned Bronze Bracers were expected to bring 6s 48c posted against 5s 7c from a vendor, and a Militant Shortsword was better vendored at 19s 16c than posted at an expected 17s 22c. Our house has no 7-day or 30-day history of its own yet, because both figures were identical on all 2,158 items, so buying cheap listings to relist is not built. A refresh script rebuilds the tool's data from fresh scans of all 24 houses in about a minute and a half.

**Cost of Merchant's Favor (Nov 10 scan):** buying an empty Waylaid Crate and its cheapest fill costs about 1s 70c to 3s a favor on our house. Cheapest was Journeyman Herbs: crate 3s 96c plus 10 Bruiseweed for 13s, for 10 favor. White Apprentice crates give 5 favor, green Apprentice and white Journeyman 10, green Journeyman 20 ([wow.gg](https://wow.gg/guides/merchants-favor)). Expert and Artisan amounts are not confirmed. The tool's Market tab keeps this current.
