# Shaman

Restoration while leveling and in dungeons, Enhancement as the second spec.

## Restoration Shaman

On Alliance, only Dwarves can be Shamans. Shamans have no Bloodlust in Forever.

Key changes:

- Riptide is the 31-point Resto talent. Since Nov 3 it has no upfront heal, only a heal over time, a 12-second cooldown and about 40% lower mana cost. It makes Chain Heal 25% stronger on the Riptide target. (Some guides still describe the old upfront heal.)

- Water Shield is the 11-point talent. Since Oct 16 it triggers on any spell crit. Beta guide: 3 orbs, each returns 2% of max mana.

- Mana Tide is the 16-point talent. Drop it early in a fight.

- Call of the Elements (level 20) drops up to four totems at once. Call of the Spirits at 30, Call of the Ancestors at 40 (Warcraft Tavern says Ancestors at 30).

- Totems: 1.0-second global cooldown, long totems last 5 minutes. Totemic Call (renamed Nov 3) moves your totems to you, 30-second cooldown. Totemic Recall destroys them and refunds 25% mana.

Group totems: since Oct 16, Grace of Air, Strength of Earth, Windfury and Flametongue reach the whole raid, and Grace of Air can run alongside Windfury or Flametongue. Resistance totems also reach the whole raid. Since Nov 10, weapon imbues and weapon totems work together: Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem can both proc on the same attack ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)).

Leveling as a group healer: Warcraft Tavern (beta) says early dungeons don't need Resto talents and suggests healing in another spec until dual spec at 40. Routine: Water Shield up, Healing Stream or Mana Spring down, Healing Wave as main heal, Lesser Healing Wave for emergencies. Legacy points can unlock talents up to 5 levels earlier.

Key talents (beta, level 30): Mindfulness, Water Shield, Mana Tide, Ancestral Healing, Tidal Mastery, Nature's Swiftness, Riptide. No level-60 build is published yet.

Stat priority (beta): Healing Power, Spirit, Intellect, Crit, Haste.

Professions (Warcraft Tavern, beta): Leatherworking for spell power leveling gear, Engineering for trinkets, Enchanting as an option. Camp buffs for a healer: Alchemy (mana regen), Herbalism (Intellect), Tailoring (Spirit).

Dwarf racials in Forever: Stoneform (10% less physical damage for 8 seconds, removes and gives immunity to bleeds, poisons and diseases, 3-minute cooldown), Artisan's Precision (+1% hit with spells and attacks), Find Treasure (works alongside other tracking, chance of extra chest items), Big Game Hunter (+5% damage to beasts). Mace and Gun Specialization and Frost Resistance are gone.

Totem quests (vanilla levels, not confirmed by Blizzard for Forever): Earth at 4 (Teo Hammerstorm, Anvilmar), Fire at 10 (Bruegs Kindleborn, Dun Morogh, continues in Loch Modan), Water at 20 (Norric Lochthane, Loch Modan, long trip), Air at 30 (Shaman trainer, leads to Arathi; rewards the Enhancement weapon Rage of the Storm). On Nov 10 Blizzard fixed inconsistent events in the final Call of Water quests, and changed Rage of the Storm to 16 Strength (was 16 Intellect) and speed 3.6 (was 3.2) ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)).

## Enhancement and dual spec

Dual spec unlocks at level 40 from the class trainer. Cost not announced (Season of Discovery charged 50g; not confirmed for Forever).

Enhancement uses two-handers. Blizzard made Two-Handed Axes and Maces baseline; no source confirms dual wield, and one guide found no Dual Wield talent in the launch tree.

Stats: Strength and Attack Power, with Windfury. On Nov 6 Blizzard said Intellect and Spell Power beat Strength and Attack Power and that hotfixes would change it. They came on Nov 10 ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)):

- Stormstrike deals 100% weapon damage (was 150%) on an 8-second cooldown (was 6).

- Mental Dexterity gives attack power equal to 33/66/100% of Intellect (was 50/100/150%).

- Maelstrom Weapon cuts cast time and mana cost by 20% per application at every rank (was 4/8/12/16/20%), and procs 6/7/8/9/10 times a minute by rank (was 20 a minute at every rank). The tooltip shows the chance for your main hand speed: 60% at 3.6 speed with 5 points, 33% at 2.0.

- Weapon imbues and weapon totems work together, so Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem both proc.

- Rage of the Storm, the Shaman quest two-hander, has 16 Strength in place of 16 Intellect and is slower (3.6).

Blizzard's note: the beta changes had pushed Enhancement toward Spell Power and Intellect gear, and these changes reverse that to favor Windfury before the raids open. No guide has published stat weights for the new numbers yet (checked Nov 11). The Shaman Sim uses the new Stormstrike and Mental Dexterity values.

Key talents (Nov 3 notes, with the Nov 10 numbers above): Stormstrike (100% weapon damage, 8-second cooldown), Mental Dexterity (attack power equal to 33/66/100% of Intellect), Flurry (up to 30% attack speed), Maelstrom Weapon (Lightning Bolt only; 6 to 10 procs a minute by rank). Rage of the Farseer is a self-only attack-speed cooldown.

Tanking: Blizzard added some tools (Spirit Weapons, Improved Stormstrike, Anticipation) but no taunt. Warcraft Tavern doubts it is enough for endgame.

Gear overlap with Resto: Intellect still counts for both through Mental Dexterity, but for a third less since Nov 10 (1 attack power per Intellect at 3 points, was 1.5). Crit is merged across melee and spells, and bonus healing gives a third as much bonus damage. Weapons are always separate, and Strength is useless for Resto.
