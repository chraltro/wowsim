No dungeon finder, no teleports, and meeting stones don't summon. Warlock summons are the only summons. Instance resets are capped at 5 per hour.

Hotfixes that touch our dungeons ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)): since Nov 9, Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs drop inside dungeons. The Drowned City's creatures were dealing about 30% more damage than intended, fixed Nov 9, and its Deathless Marrow pack was toned down. Nov 9 also made "Croaky's Head" and similar head quests available to every party member.

## Dungeon XP and quests

Dungeon mob XP is cut on every run, including the first, but less than at launch: on Nov 9 Blizzard raised XP from all dungeon kills by 50% ([Blizzard hotfixes, Nov 5-10](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)). Dungeon quests pay about double Classic and can only be done once, so the first clear with all quests is where the XP is. Aggrend (Josh Greenfield) says pure dungeon grinding will stay "very close to optimal questing at best".

| Date       | Change                                                                                                                                   |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nov 9      | Hotfix: XP from every enemy kill in dungeons up 50%                                                                                      |
| Nov 2      | Launch notes: dungeon XP unchanged                                                                                                       |
| Oct 8      | Kill XP up about 20%. Party XP now based on your own level. If someone in the party is vastly higher level than the mob, nobody gets XP. |
| Oct 1      | Extra quest XP on top of Classic cut by 50%                                                                                              |
| Beta start | Mob XP cut (Method: about 70% below Classic; Blizzard only said "way down"), quest XP boosted                                            |

Examples: Deviate Hides (Wailing Caverns) 1,600 XP in Classic, 3,100 in Forever. Deadmines quests 8,300 in Classic, 18,450 in Forever (includes a new quest).

Group XP: in a full five-man group each player gets 28% of a solo kill's XP (split five ways, times 1.4). Quest XP is not split. Method's advice: quest through a zone, end with its dungeon, collect and share quests before going in.

Alliance quest pickups:

- Hall of Thanes: Thom Filch by the Ironforge entrance bridge (Important Heirlooms). Earthseer Farsen at Gol'Bolar Quarry (Old Ironforge Incursion, after Nip 'Em in the Bud and a Dark Iron Map from a Dark Iron Spy). Inside: The Restless Dead, An Ancient Grudge. The Treaty of Understanding tablet in the last boss room goes to Magni.

- Ruins of Lordaeron: all four Alliance quests start inside.

- Deadmines: Wilder Thistlenettle and Shoni in Stormwind's Dwarven District; Gryan Stoutmantle, Scout Riell and Alba Fairmoon at Sentinel Hill. VanCleef's Unsent Letter goes to Baros Alexston in Stormwind.

- Excavation Site: Dorin Songblade (Lakeshire), Caitlin Grassman and James Halloran (Menethil), Rethiel the Greenwarden (three-quest chain). Final boss Titan Relic goes to Prospector Whelgar, then High Explorer Magellas in Ironforge.

- City of Dalaran: Andromath (Mage Quarter) and Shylamiir (the Park) in Stormwind; Image of Archmage Modera at the sewer entrance, after An Alarming Request.

- Drowned City: Magics of the Drowned City, Take Two With Water (givers not found).
