# Changes

What changed in the game and in these pages, newest first.

## 2026-11-12

- **Gold: the house on Nov 12.** Low-zone goods now cost several times more on our house than elsewhere in the EU (Pristine Leather, Savory Whimsyfin Delight, Stranglekelp, Spider's Silk), Swiftthistle has doubled on PvP rulesets, Waylaid Crates have fallen, and the Darkmoon Faire (Nov 9 to 15, Elwynn) lifted Small Furry Paw. Added drop chances for Swiftthistle, Stranglekelp and Frilled Lichen, the Whimsyfin facts, Blueprint prices and the vendor flips. See [Gold making](gold.md).
- **What one kill sells for.** Valued every level 9 to 25 creature in Alliance zones from Classic drop chances and the Nov 12 prices: coyotes, goretusks, vultures and crawlers lead at low level, everything else is under 1s a kill.
- **Gold at low level.** Added Coyote Meat (6s, cooks need it), where the cheap recipes are sold, and the beta finding that first-tier Scarce materials may stop dropping after level 25 (unconfirmed, and the big houses' prices argue against it).

## 2026-11-11

Read Blizzard's hotfix notes for Nov 5, 6, 9 and 10 ([source](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-10-2026)) and brought every page in line.

- **Enhancement was turned back toward Strength and Windfury (Nov 10).** Stormstrike 100% weapon damage on 8 seconds (was 150% on 6). Mental Dexterity 33/66/100% of Intellect (was 50/100/150%). Maelstrom Weapon 20% per application at every rank and 6 to 10 procs a minute by rank. Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem now both proc. Rage of the Storm has Strength in place of Intellect. See [Shaman](shaman.md).
- **Dungeon kill XP up 50% (Nov 9).** Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs now drop in dungeons. See [Leveling path and dungeons](leveling.md).
- **Auction deposits: 1 copper minimum (Nov 10), was 1 silver.** Vendor prices cut for some crafted armor, Iron Grenades and Minor Arcane Elixir. Most quests from level 48 pay gold. See [Gold making](gold.md).
- **Professions:** Journeyman Campfire Kit at Cooking 90 (was 140), Tanning Rack at Leatherworking 125 (was 140), Coarse Grinding Stone gives points to 90. See [Systems](systems.md).
- **Other classes:** Warlock pets tougher and Wrack reworked, Hunter pet health corrected down to 115%, Arcane Concentration split across bolts. See [What each class brings](classes.md).
- The Shaman Sim now uses the Nov 10 Stormstrike and Mental Dexterity numbers. In a level 60 healing set the hybrid build does about two thirds more melee damage than the pure healer, where it did about twice as much before.
- The Group Sim's engine is still the one from before Nov 10, so its Enhancement, Hunter pet and Warlock numbers are the old ones. The engine's authors have the changes; taking them means redoing six of our nine fixes against their new code (see `docs/multiclass.md`).
- Item changes from hotfixes now live in `data/hotfixes.json` and are laid over the item files at build time. First entry: Rage of the Storm.
- The knowledge bank was split from one file into these pages.
