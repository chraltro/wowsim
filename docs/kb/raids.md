# Raids and roadmap

The three launch raids and what Blizzard has announced after them.

All three launch raids unlock on Dec 9, 2026, need level 60 and reset weekly. Whether you can enter with fewer players than the size is unconfirmed. The group of five needs five more for the smallest raid.

| Raid          | Size | Bosses       | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|---------------|------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Barrow Deeps  | 10   | 8            | Entrances via Mount Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold                                                                                                                                                       |
| Hyjal Summit  | 20   | 13           | Opens on an ancient guardian, then splits into routes above and below ground; final boss The Wild King                                                                                                          |
| Onyxia's Lair | 40   | 1 (3 phases) | Needs the Drakefire Amulet in your bags. Alliance chain starts with Helendis Riverhorn in Burning Steppes, runs through BRD, Stormwind and Bolvar, ends with General Drakkisath in UBRS. Bring fire resistance. |

Every raid boss has one to three rare chase items (Kris Zierhut). No official loot or tier set details yet.

Roadmap (Blizzard's image via Blizzard Watch):

- Hardcore realms: after launch, no date (secondhand reports say winter).

- Spring 2027: two new raids (10 and 20), two dungeons, a new zone, a legendary questline, PvP season refresh.

- Summer 2027: a revamped classic raid, a new raid, two dungeons, expanded world content, PvP refresh, profession and Legacy updates.

- Player housing has been teased.
